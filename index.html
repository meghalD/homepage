Meghal Dani

I'm a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the International Max Planck Research School for Intelligent Systems, University of Tübingen, Germany. I work at the intersection of computer vision, large language models, and clinical neuroscience, with a focus on building reliable models for epilepsy and brain data. I am also a guest PhD student at Helmholtz-Munich and Technical University of Munich (TUM).

I am part of ClinBrAIn, a clinical neuroscience initiative in Tübingen, and am advised by Dr. rer. nat. Stefanie Liebe and Prof. Dr. Jakob Macke. Previously, I worked with Prof. Dr. Zeynep Akata on vision–language models and explainable AI and, in the first year of my Ph.D., at the MIDAS lab with Prof. Dr. Med. Sergios Gatidis.

My journey combines computer science and biology: I received my Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Science from BIT Mesra in 2016 and focused on AI in healthcare during my Masters in Computational Biology (2017–2019) at IIIT Delhi. I then worked as a full-time researcher (2019–2021) at Tata Research and Innovation Labs on deep learning and 3D vision with Dr. Lovekesh Vig and Ramya Hebbalaguppe.

I enjoy projects where models have to work on messy, real-world clinical data: handwritten notes, long EEG recordings, low-quality hospital videos and multi-center MRI cohorts. My work spans lesion detection in CT, compact 3D pose estimation, epilepsy semiology modeling and EEG foundation models. I am actively looking for research internship / visiting researcher positions for summer 2026 (industry or academic labs) in foundation models, multimodal learning, and safe/robust ML for healthcare.
